Highway

      
Night Runner’s highway is modelled after “[FIXED] New York, Highway Interstate 95” by 99.Miles (Sketchfab Standard license). Its concrete, asphalt, barrier, rock, grass, stone, steel, water and tree textures are used here, resized for the web.

      
The endless road itself, the bridges, causeways, signs, streetlights, night sky, wet-road and water reflections are generated in code for this game.

Sound

      
Supra: the idle is cut from RacingSystems’ “Supra 1000HP Idle sound”; engine, burnout, turbo and shift sounds are cut from three Toyota Supra recordings supplied for this game.

      
Corvette Z06: engine sound cut from NM2255’s “2016 Chevrolet Corvette C7 Z06 Exhaust Sound – Start Up, Revs & Accelerations” (NM2255 | Raw Car Sounds). Idle chop from Late Model Racecraft’s “LMR1000HP C7 Corvette IDLE CHOP” (Late Model Racecraft – LMR), which also chops under low-rev acceleration. Extra saturation and the supercharger whine are added in code.

      
GT-R: for now it borrows the M4 CSL’s twin-turbo engine recording below, with more turbo and blow-off sound.

      
S2000: engine sound cut from Valvetronic Designs’ “The WORLD’S BEST SOUNDING HONDA S2000? - 8,300 RPM NEVER SOUNDED BETTER!” (Valvetronic Designs), used with permission: the idle, a long pull to 7,300 rpm and the overrun of a free rev.

      
BMW i8: engine sound cut from Janky Whips’ “I Disabled Active Sound On My BMW i8 And The Sound Of The Stock Exhaust Might Surprise You” (@jankywhips1756), used with permission.

      
Civic Type R and Eclipse: for now they borrow the M4 CSL’s recording below too, with more turbo. The police Charger borrows the Corvette’s.

      
Viper ACR: engine sound cut from AUTO BILD SPORTSCARS’ “2016 Dodge Viper ACR - Acceleration & High Speed Runs (Autobahn)” (filmed with GeigerCars Munich), GommeBlog’s “2016 Dodge Viper ACR | EXHAUST SOUND and ACCELERATION” and 951 Hellgato’s “EXHAUST REVS 2016 DODGE VIPER ACR STOCK !”: onboard pulls, track overrun, and the idle and revs of a stock car. The odd-fire beat is restored in code on the four quietest loops; the idle’s rasp above 250 Hz is borrowed from the Corvette’s idle recording below (the two fire at almost the same rate), since the street capture carried only the rumble.

      
Carrera GT: engine sound cut from NM2255’s “Porsche Carrera GT SCREAMING V10 Sounds! - Accelerations, Revs, OnBoard, Exhaust & More!” and “BEST OF Porsche Carrera GT 5.7 N/A V10 Engine SOUNDS! - OnBoard, Revs, Burnouts & More!” (NM2255 | Raw Car Sounds): the idle at the tailpipes, onboard full-throttle pulls to 8,000 rpm and onboard overrun, played back at the revs they were recorded at.

      
M4 CSL: engine sound cut from NW Auto’s “F82 BMW M4 Stock Exhaust Sound”, used with permission. Its turbo and shift sounds reuse the Supra recordings, turned well down.

      
Aventador SVJ: engine sound cut from AutoTopNL’s “Lamborghini Aventador SVJ *Gintani Exhaust* V12 F1 SOUND!” (AutoTopNL), used with permission: the idle, a roadside pull to 7,400 rpm and the overrun.

      
M3 E92: no recording of its own yet; it borrows the Corvette’s V8, above.

      
Skyline R34: no recording of its own yet; it borrows the Supra’s straight six, above.

      
GR Supra: no recording of its own yet; it borrows the M4’s straight six, above.

      
812 Superfast and Competizione: engine sound cut from NM2255’s “Novitec straight piped Ferrari 812 GTS sounds INCREDIBLE on the DYNO | 9000rpm SCREAMING V12 Eargasm” (NM2255 | Raw Car Sounds), used with permission: the idle, a dyno pull from 2,400 to 8,800 rpm and the fall of the revs after it.

      
911 GT3 RS: engine sound cut from Automann-TV’s “Porsche 991.2 GT3 RS (520hp) - pure SOUND” (Automann-TV), used with permission. The recording is the 991.2 GT3 RS, whose 4.0-litre flat six the 992’s is developed from: its idle, pulls through the gears to 7,900 rpm, free revs and overrun.

      
720S Spider: engine sound cut from JBB013’s “McLaren 720S Brutal SOUND - Accelerations and Loud Revs” (JBB013 - Supercar Videos), used with permission: the idle, a full-throttle pull to 7,800 rpm and the crackle of the free revs.

      
Gear changes are made in code for each car from its own engine sound: how far and how fast the revs fall, a manual’s lift or a dual-clutch’s ignition cut, the gearbox clunks and the downshift blip. The blow-off on the turbo cars is the Supra recording’s, tuned per car.
